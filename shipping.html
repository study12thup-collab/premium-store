Shipping & Delivery Policy

Delivery charges

Delivery is free on all orders across India. There are no hidden shipping or handling fees.

Cash on Delivery (COD)

COD is available for orders up to ₹3,000, with a flat COD handling charge of ₹49 added to the order total (shown at checkout before you place the order). Please keep the exact amount ready in cash when the parcel arrives. COD orders are confirmed immediately and shipped within 1–2 business days. Repeated refusal of COD parcels may lead to COD being disabled for that phone number or address.

Processing time

Online-payment orders are processed within 1–2 business days after payment is confirmed and are not processed until payment is successful.

Delivery time

Most orders are delivered within 3–7 business days after dispatch, depending on your location. Remote areas may take longer.

Tracking

Once your order is shipped, the courier / tracking details are added to your order. Use Track Order on our store with the email you ordered with to see the latest status (Order Placed → Processing → Shipped → Delivered).

Delivery address

Please enter a complete and correct address and pincode. We are not responsible for delays or failed delivery caused by an incorrect or incomplete address. If you made a mistake, call us on +91 8318362335 as soon as possible, before the order ships.

Delays

Weather, strikes, festivals or courier issues can occasionally delay delivery. If your order is late, contact us and we will follow up with the courier.
