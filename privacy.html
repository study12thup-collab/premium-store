Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Premium Store collects and how we use it. Last updated: 13 October 2026.

Information we collect

  • Order details you enter at checkout: name, email, mobile number, delivery address, city, state and pincode.
  • Order information: products, quantities, amount and order status.
  • Basic technical data your browser sends (such as device and browser type).

Payments

Payments are processed by Cashfree Payments. We do not see or store your card, UPI or bank details. Cashfree shares only the payment status and reference with us.

How we use your information

  • To process, ship and deliver your order and to send order updates.
  • To provide customer support, returns and refunds.
  • To prevent fraud and keep the store secure.

Sharing

We share your name, phone number and address only with delivery partners / suppliers who need them to deliver your order, and with payment and hosting providers who help us run the store. We do not sell your personal information.

Data storage and security

Your order data is stored with our database provider (Supabase) with access restricted to the store owner. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no online system is 100% secure.
