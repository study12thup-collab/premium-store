Return, Refund & Cancellation Policy

Easy 7-day returns

You can request a return within 7 days of delivery if the item is damaged, defective, wrong, or significantly not as described.

Conditions

  • The product must be unused, in original condition and packaging, with all tags and accessories.
  • Please share clear photos / a short unboxing video of the issue when you contact us.
  • Items that are used, damaged by the customer, or missing parts are not eligible.
  • Innerwear, personal-care and consumable products cannot be returned once opened, unless defective.
  • For a damaged or wrong parcel, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos / an unboxing video.

How to request a return

Call us on +91 8318362335 within 7 days of delivery with your Order ID and details of the issue. We will confirm and arrange pickup or tell you where to send the item.

Refunds

Once the returned item is received and checked, your refund is issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days (your bank may take a few extra days to show it). If we sent a wrong or damaged item, we will send a replacement or refund in full, including any return shipping cost.

Cancellation

You can cancel an order before it is shipped by calling us on +91 8318362335 with your Order ID. A full refund is issued to the original payment method. Once shipped, the return policy applies.

Returns on Cash on Delivery orders

The same 7-day return policy applies. Since the payment was made in cash, the refund is sent by bank transfer / UPI to the account details you share with us, within 5–7 business days after the returned item is checked.

Failed or double payments

If money was deducted but your order was not confirmed, Cashfree usually refunds it automatically within a few business days. If it doesn't arrive, contact us with the payment details and we will help.
